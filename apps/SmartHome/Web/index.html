Photos, video and music

      
      
      

      

      

      

      
Plays files from this PC on DLNA players and Google Cast devices. AirPlay 2 devices work through the pyatv tool (pair once with atvremote wizard); the older AirPlay method is best effort. The volume buttons work on Google Cast only. For Cast, MP4 (H.264) and MP3 are the safest; MKV and MOV often will not play. The first time, Windows may ask to allow the helper on your private network. Choose Allow.

🔒 About these logins

      
These two talk to GE's and Hubspace's servers on the internet (everything else in this app stays on your home network). Your password is only used once to sign in and is never saved; the mini PC keeps the login key the service sends back (cync-token.json, hubspace-token.json). Best done on the mini PC itself. Opening the Cync phone app briefly disconnects this app from Cync; it reconnects by itself.
